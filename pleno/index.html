Privacy & data

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Your location stays on your iPhone. To fetch prices, Pleno only asks the public sources for the area you're looking at.

No account

Open the app and go. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to remember.

No ads

Just prices. No banners, no sponsored stations.

No tracking

No analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no personal data on any server.

Location stays on device

Only the search area is sent to the official price services, never linked to you.
