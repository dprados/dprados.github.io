Why Turnada

What other shift apps don't do

Built for real shift work: duties that run past midnight, pay agreements full of fine print, and rosters that keep changing.

  • A 24-hour duty on a holiday counts for what it isBonuses can be worked out by blocks of hours: if 16 hours of the duty fall on a holiday, that's 2 holidays.
  • Pay rises without rewriting the pastSet the date of the rise and earlier months keep their old rates.
  • Overtime with three ratesDaytime, night or holiday, and night and holiday. Every hour is paid the way it should be.
  • An hours balance that starts when you doVacation and sick leave credited, target changes during the year, and carry-over from last year.
  • Rotations that don't stop in DecemberYour cycle runs for years. No setting it up again every January.
  • Your paper roster, on your phone from a photoText recognition happens on the phone itself: the photo never gets uploaded.
  • Shift swaps with clear accountsWho owes you a shift and who you owe one to, without a notebook.
  • You're on iPhone, your partner's on AndroidShare rosters all the same and see the days you're both off.
  • Switch a bonus off for just one dayThat holiday wasn't paid? Turn it off for that day and the rest of the month stays the same.
  • No account, no ads, no trackingYour shifts and your pay never leave your phone (or your own iCloud).
