Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Wordsoup Daily ("we", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects on iPhone, iPad and Android, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Data stored on your device

Your game data (progress, preferences, coins, streaks, completed puzzles) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data and we do not operate servers that store it.

1.2 Backup and sync

iOS: if iCloud is enabled, your game progress is stored in your personal iCloud account via CloudKit, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Android: your game progress is included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

We do not access your iCloud or Google backup data.

1.3 Leaderboards

If you sign in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), your player ID is used to submit daily scores to leaderboards. This is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

1.4 On-device AI

The AI puzzle creator runs entirely on your device: Apple Intelligence on iOS, Gemini Nano (Google AICore / ML Kit) on supported Android devices. Your themes and the generated words are not sent to any server.

1.5 Data collected by third-party advertising

For free users, ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may collect the following data to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads:

Data typeLinked to youUsed for tracking
Device or advertising identifierYesYes (only with your consent)
Advertising data (ads viewed and tapped)NoYes (only with your consent)
Product interactionNoNo
Coarse location (derived from IP address)NoNo
Diagnostics (ad SDK performance and crashes)NoNo

iOS: personalized ads are only shown if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. Android: where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; the Android advertising ID can be reset or deleted at any time in your device's settings. If you do not consent, Google only serves non-personalized ads.

1.6 Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and linked to your Apple ID or Google account. We check the purchase status to unlock features, but we never receive or store your payment information.

1.7 Word definitions

When you tap a word to see its definition, iOS shows it from the dictionaries installed on your device. On Android the app opens Wiktionary in your browser, which sends that word to Wikimedia under the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.

1.8 Notifications

Daily and streak reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to a notification server.

2. How We Use Data

  • App functionality: to save and back up your progress and manage premium features.
  • Advertising: to serve ads to free users via Google AdMob, as described above.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or any other personal information. We do not operate any servers that store your personal data, and we do not sell your data.

4. Your Choices

  • Tracking (iOS): decline the ATT prompt when first asked, or change it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Ads consent (Android): where shown, choose your options in Google's consent form; reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Ads: buy Premium to remove all advertisements.
  • Backup: turn iCloud (iOS) or Google backup (Android) on or off in your device settings.
  • Delete data: uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Backups can be deleted from your iCloud storage settings or your Google account.

5. Children's Privacy

Wordsoup Daily is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform — advertising and consent
  • Apple (iCloud, Game Center, StoreKit, Apple Intelligence) — sync, leaderboards, purchases and on-device AI on iOS
  • Google (Android backup, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, ML Kit / Gemini Nano) — backup, leaderboards, purchases and on-device AI on Android
  • Wikimedia (Wiktionary) — word definitions on Android

7. Data Security

Your game data is stored on your device or in your personal iCloud or Google backup. We do not transmit or store your data on our own servers. Third-party services use industry-standard encryption, and all their network traffic is encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy, contact us at dpappssupport@gmail.com.
